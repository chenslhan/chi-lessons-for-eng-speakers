About Me

I focus on helping adult learners build practical Mandarin skills through structured, level-based lessons.

Teaching focus

  • Clear level progression (Beginner → Intermediate)
  • Pronunciation and tone accuracy
  • Useful real-world conversation
  • Step-by-step grammar that supports speaking confidence

Lesson experience

Lessons are tailored to your goals (travel, daily life, work, social conversations). We use structured practice to build fluency and confidence over time.

You can start as a complete beginner or take a short placement test if you have prior experience.

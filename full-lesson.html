Book a Full 50-Minute Lesson

The 50-minute lesson is the standard lesson format for students who wish to make consistent progress in Mandarin Chinese.

Lesson Details

  • Duration: 50 minutes
  • Format: One-to-one online lesson
  • Availability: Flexible scheduling across time zones

What a full lesson may include

  • Guided speaking and listening practice
  • Clear explanations in English where needed
  • Structured progression by level
  • Review and reinforcement
  • Homework or follow-up practice where appropriate

Placement Test (If Applicable)

  • Absolute beginners do not need to take a placement test.
  • Students with prior Chinese background are required to complete a short placement test (15 minutes or less).

The placement test link is available on the Registration page.

Lesson Bundles

Lesson bundles are available for students who wish to book multiple sessions.

  • Offer better value per lesson
  • Support consistent learning progress
  • Reduce repeated booking and payment steps

Full bundle information is available on the Payment Information page.

Payment

Lessons are conducted after payment has been received. Select your preferred payment method on the Payment Information page and proceed accordingly.

How My Lessons Work

To maintain a committed environment, my lessons operate on a pre-paid enrollment model.

MANDATORY: Payments are required before any bookings will be registered. Bookings without payment will be ignored.
1

Enrollment

Beginners can start from scratch and start booking lessons immediately after payment is made. Those with prior Chinese experience are required to take a 15-min check to ensure we don't waste time on material that is inappropriate for you.

2

Scheduling

Students can immediately make bookings on my calendar directly whether they have paid or not. However, I will only conduct lessons with those who have actually made the payment. Bookings without payment will be ignored.

3

Learning

We meet via your preferred online mode (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, WhatsApp, WeChat, or Line). I do not teach in-person lessons.

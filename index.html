How My Lessons Work

MANDATORY: Payments are required before any bookings will be registered.
1

Secure Your Spot

Secure Your Spot with Making Payment of a Single lesson or lesson bundle. You can purchase a Single lesson or lesson bundle by clicking the Pricing & Bundles button above.

2

Beginners Start Right Away

If you’re new to Chinese, you can begin learning from scratch. After payment, you’ll be able to book your lessons immediately via the Book 50-min lesson button above.

3

Prior Experience Gets a Quick Level Calibration

If you’ve studied Chinese before, after making payment you are invited for a short 15-minute level calibration so we can start at the appropriate pace and content level. This step helps avoid situations where lessons feel too easy or too difficult for you. You can find the level calibration button above.

*NOTE*: You wont be able to commence studies for levels higher than low beginner before completing the level calibration.

4

Then Book Your Lessons

After the level calibration is completed, you may book your lessons via the Book 50-min lesson button above.

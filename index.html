How My Lessons Work

To maintain a committed environment, my lessons operate on a pre-paid enrollment model.

Enrollment

Beginners can start from scratch and start booking lessons immediately after payment is made. Those with prior Chinese experience are required to take a 15-min check to ensure we don't waste time on material that is inappropriate for you.

2

Scheduling

Once your payment is confirmed, you will receive a private booking link. Simply pick a time slot that works for you. I offer 24/7 flexibility to match your time zone.

3

Learning

We meet via your preferred mode (Zoom, Google Meet, or In-person if local). Lessons are interactive and focus on practical English-to-Chinese speaking skills.

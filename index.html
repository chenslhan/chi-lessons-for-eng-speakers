How lessons work

Structure

  • Level-based progression so you always know what to do next
  • Guided speaking, listening, and practice activities
  • Homework or follow-up practice where appropriate

Placement test

Absolute beginners do not need to take a placement test.

Students with prior Chinese background are required to complete a short placement test (15 minutes or less) to ensure lessons start at the correct level.

The placement test link is provided on the Registration page.

Flexible scheduling across time zones

Students may book lessons based on their own local time zone. Booking times are displayed automatically in your local time.

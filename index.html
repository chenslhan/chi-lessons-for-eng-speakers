How My Lessons Work

MANDATORY: Payments are required before any bookings will be registered.
1. Enrollment

Secure your spot by making a payment for a single lesson or lesson bundle via the Pricing page.

2. Scheduling

Beginners Start Right Away

If you're new to Chinese, you can begin learning from scratch. After payment, please register your details via the Lesson Registration Form button.

After registering, you'll be able to book your lessons immediately via the Book 50-min lesson button above. See you in class !!

3. Calibration

Learners with Prior Chinese Background will need Quick Level calibration.

If you’ve studied Chinese before, after payment I invite you to do a short 15–minute level calibration so we can start at the appropriate pace and content level.

*NOTE*: You wont be able to commence studies for levels higher than low beginner before completing the level calibration.

4. After Calibration

Then Book Your Lessons

After the level calibration is completed, please register your details via the Lesson Registration Form button.

After registering, you'll be able to book your lessons immediately via the Book 50-min lesson button above. See you in class !!

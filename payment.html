Payment Policy

Lessons are conducted after payment has been received.

This applies to trial lessons, single lessons, and lesson bundles.

  • Trial lesson: 30 minutes · SGD 10 (one per student)
  • Full lesson: 50 minutes · SGD 50

If you are unsure whether to start with a trial or a full lesson, the trial lesson is a low-cost way to experience the lesson style and pace.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Trial Lessons: Trial lessons are non-refundable.

Bundle Packages: I want you to be 100% confident in your learning journey. If you change your mind for any reason, I offer a 7-day cooling-off period for all bundle purchases. As long as you haven't started your lessons, I will provide a full refund, no questions asked.

Partial Refunds: If you wish to discontinue after starting a bundle, unused lessons will be refunded at the remaining value, calculated by charging the full single-lesson rate for all completed sessions.

Expiration: All lesson bundles must be used within 6 months (for 5–10 lessons) or 12 months (for 20+ lessons) from the date of purchase.

24-Hour Notice: Booked lessons cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice will be charged in full.
